# AE Enchanting Table

<BlockImage id="applied_more_machines:ae_enchanting_table" scale="6" />

Insert equipment or a normal book, choose enchantments, then press the button below the item slot. Normal books become anvil-ready enchanted books. Enchanted books cannot be inserted.

## Controls

- **Left-click an enchantment** to select it, then add one level per click. **Shift + left-click** sets its current maximum.
- **Right-click** subtracts one level, or deselects at level 1. **Shift + right-click** sets level 1, or deselects if already at level 1.
- The bottom-right arrows also adjust the focused enchantment. Conflicting entries are disabled; level limits follow the current game rules.

Existing enchantments are preserved. An upgrade's minimum new target is its existing level plus one; reducing further cancels that upgrade.

## Energy

Each **added level costs 8,000 AE**. Existing levels are free. No experience or lapis is consumed.

The buffer stores **256,000 AE** and operation requires **one active ME channel**. All six faces accept ME/FE power; item automation is unavailable.
Larger jobs pay across refills. Items and choices stay locked during payment, which pauses without a channel. Paid progress is saved; enchanting finishes immediately when fully paid.
