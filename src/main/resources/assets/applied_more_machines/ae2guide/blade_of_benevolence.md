# Blade of Benevolence

Forged from every player's hatred of the Chaos Guardian.

Available with Draconic Evolution installed. Uses the vanilla wooden sword model, stacks to one, and never loses durability.

## Use

Attacks against ordinary entities produce only a swing, with no damage, knockback, fire or hurt reaction. Hitting the Draconic Evolution Chaos Guardian or any of its body parts directly invokes the guardian's kill logic.

## Crafting

Place four Draconium Ingots in the corners, a Dragon Heart at the top center, three Draconium Cores at the left, right and bottom center, and a wooden sword in the center.
