# Pickaxe of Righteousness

Forged from every player's ingenuity in bypassing the Chaos Crystal.

Available with Draconic Evolution installed. Uses the vanilla wooden pickaxe model, stacks to one, and never loses durability.

## Use

Cannot break any other block, including instant-break grass and mushrooms. In survival, continuously mine a Chaos Crystal for approximately **one second (20 ticks)**, even while its guardian is alive.

The protection bypass exists only during this crystal removal. It does not change the guardian-defeated flag. Crystal-part cleanup and chaos-shard drops use Draconic Evolution's normal logic and configuration. Other tools retain the original restrictions.

## Crafting

Place four Draconium Ingots in the corners, a Dragon Heart at the top center, three Draconium Cores at the left, right and bottom center, and a wooden pickaxe in the center.
